Why I'm Running

I am running because I believe our politics needs a renewed commitment to practical solutions, evidence-based policymaking, and respectful dialogue.

Too often, political debates have become defined by division and ideology rather than by careful consideration of what actually works for the people we represent.

I believe in a tradition of Democratic governance that values opportunity, responsibility, and improving the lives of ordinary citizens. I want to bring that approach back to Manchester and to the State House.
